This for me is the definition of reading a blockbuster. It's fast paced, action packed and fun. In the beginning I feared it might be a bit one note but later in the book the mystery around the dungeon and 
its creators opens up a lot more which keeps you invested. 

The humour is a bit to meme-y for my taste at some points. Also, if you are not familiar with the world of (modern) gaming you will be pretty much lost.
