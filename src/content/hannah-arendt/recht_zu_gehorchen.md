The topics of the interviews are adjacent to each other. The first illuminates how Hannah Arendt views the german people and the intellectual circles she was a part of during and after the 3rd Reich while the second is more focussed on her book about Eichmann. Her thoughts on the banality of evil and the credo which is the title of the book ("No human has the right to obey") gave me a lot to think about regarding one's own role in history. The lower rating is mostly because of the price for relatively short interviews that could also be found elsewhere, not for the content of these interviews.
