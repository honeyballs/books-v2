This book is the most cruel and vile installment of the series so far. It adds a lot of lore and depth to the already complex world of the Malazan series. The backstories and motivations of the cast become clearer and more fleshed out while the main plot stays exciting and incredibly dark. I only disliked a passage where one of the characters gave a run down of his abilities which felt a bit like a video game tutorial.
