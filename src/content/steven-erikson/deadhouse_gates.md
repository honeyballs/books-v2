It's brutal, dark and cruel. Hope is rare to come by in this world and its inhabitants suffer at every step of the way. But it is also quite captivating.

The book fleshes out the universe of the Malazan saga a lot more with new lands and plots concerning mostly different characters than in the first installment. I am curious and excited to find out more.
