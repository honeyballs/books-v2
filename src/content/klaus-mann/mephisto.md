Mephisto was written 90 years ago but it is painfully incredibly current. It tells the story of a theatre actor who disregards his friends and his believes to further his career within 1930s Germany.

The characterizations Mann uses to describe the figures in his novel are very pointed and precise but timeless. He manages to capture the essence of various degrees of participation in a fascist regime. 
Reading this book today and comparing it to the political climate and actions of Germany now in the year 2026 hurts, because it makes it clear that we find ourselves at this exact point in history once again. 

I cannot recommend reading this novel enough - if you can stomach the sense of dread that comes with it. It forces you to think a lot about the kind of person you want to be in the face of overwhelming evil.
