The book covers a wide range of philosophical topics and relates them well to Drag Race. While it does not provide a deep discussion of individual topics since it is a collection of shorter essays there are still a lot of thought provoking and fun ideas especially related to gender, self expression, authenticity, freedom and even death. Unfortunately some points are a bit repetitive because the different authors of the essays share thoughts and explain things and concepts again that also were covered in another essay.
